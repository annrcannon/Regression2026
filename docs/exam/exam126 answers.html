Exam Scenario

The data read in below consists of a sample of 386 African-American patients. The data was collected to research the relationship among a number of predictor variables and whether the patient had been diagnosed with diabetes or not. Among the possible predictors are: chol (cholesterol), stab.glu (stabilized glucose), hdl (hdl cholesterol), ratio (chol/hdl), glyhb(glycosolated hemoglopin - a measure of the amount of sugar in the blood), bp.1s (first systolic blood pressure), bp.1d (first diastolic blood pressure), hip (hip measurement), and time.ppn (number of minutes after eating, when blood was taken). We will consider the analysis of whether people are diagnosed with diabetes or not in this problem.

For this exam we will NOT be using the variable glyhb because it is essentially used to diagnose diabetes.
